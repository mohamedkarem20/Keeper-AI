# 🚀 Keeper-AI — Customer Churn Prediction Platform

An end-to-end **AI-powered Customer Churn Prediction Platform** built with **Machine Learning, NLP, Explainable AI, and Streamlit** to predict customer retention risk and provide actionable business insights.

Keeper-AI transforms customer data into intelligent decisions through an automated ML pipeline, interactive analytics dashboard, churn prediction engine, and explainable AI capabilities.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges facing businesses, especially in e-commerce and subscription-based industries.

Losing customers directly impacts revenue, customer lifetime value, and business growth.

Keeper-AI helps organizations:

- Identify customers at high risk of churn
- Predict churn probability
- Understand the main factors behind customer decisions
- Analyze customer sentiment using NLP
- Generate data-driven retention strategies

The platform combines Machine Learning, NLP, and Business Analytics into a production-style AI application.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Companies need an intelligent system that can answer:

- Which customers are likely to leave?
- What factors contribute to customer churn?
- How can businesses take preventive actions?

The objective of Keeper-AI is to build an AI solution capable of:

1. Predicting customer churn.
2. Estimating churn probability scores.
3. Explaining model decisions.
4. Providing actionable customer retention insights.
